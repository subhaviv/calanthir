SUMMARY OF BENEFITS AND COVERAGE (SBC)
PPO Plan — Plan Year 2026
Coverage Period: 01/01/2026 – 12/31/2026

This is a Preferred Provider Organization (PPO) plan. You do NOT need a referral to see a specialist.

===========================================================================
DEDUCTIBLES
===========================================================================
Individual Deductible (In-Network):       $1,500 per plan year
Family Deductible (In-Network):           $3,000 per plan year
Individual Deductible (Out-of-Network):   $3,000 per plan year
Family Deductible (Out-of-Network):       $6,000 per plan year

The family deductible is met once covered members collectively reach $3,000 in-network,
even if no individual member has met their individual deductible.

===========================================================================
OUT-OF-POCKET MAXIMUMS
===========================================================================
Individual Out-of-Pocket Maximum (In-Network):     $4,500 per plan year
Family Out-of-Pocket Maximum (In-Network):         $9,000 per plan year
Individual Out-of-Pocket Maximum (Out-of-Network): $9,000 per plan year
Family Out-of-Pocket Maximum (Out-of-Network):     $18,000 per plan year

Once the out-of-pocket maximum is met, the plan pays 100% of covered in-network services
for the remainder of the plan year. Premiums do not count toward the out-of-pocket maximum.

===========================================================================
MEDICAL COST-SHARE (AFTER DEDUCTIBLE UNLESS NOTED)
===========================================================================

PRIMARY CARE VISIT (In-Network):        $30 copay per visit
PRIMARY CARE VISIT (Out-of-Network):    40% coinsurance

SPECIALIST VISIT (In-Network):          $60 copay per visit
SPECIALIST VISIT (Out-of-Network):      40% coinsurance

URGENT CARE CENTER (In-Network):        $75 copay per visit
URGENT CARE CENTER (Out-of-Network):    40% coinsurance

EMERGENCY ROOM:                         $350 copay per visit (waived if admitted)
  Note: ER copay applies regardless of in-network or out-of-network status for
  true medical emergencies. The copay is waived if the member is admitted as inpatient.

INPATIENT HOSPITAL (In-Network):        20% coinsurance per admission (after deductible)
INPATIENT HOSPITAL (Out-of-Network):    40% coinsurance per admission

OUTPATIENT SURGERY (In-Network):        20% coinsurance (after deductible)
OUTPATIENT SURGERY (Out-of-Network):    40% coinsurance

OUTPATIENT LAB / PATHOLOGY (In-Network):   $20 copay per service
OUTPATIENT IMAGING / X-RAY (In-Network):   $50 copay per service
ADVANCED IMAGING / MRI / CT (In-Network):  20% coinsurance (after deductible)
ADVANCED IMAGING (Out-of-Network):         40% coinsurance

===========================================================================
PREVENTIVE CARE
===========================================================================
ACA-mandated preventive services (annual wellness exam, recommended screenings,
immunizations, contraceptive services) are covered at $0 cost-share in-network,
with no deductible requirement, when billed as preventive.

If a preventive visit results in the diagnosis or treatment of a medical condition,
additional charges may apply at the standard cost-share.

===========================================================================
MENTAL HEALTH AND SUBSTANCE USE DISORDER (MHSUD)
===========================================================================
Outpatient Mental Health Visit (In-Network):        $30 copay per visit
Outpatient Mental Health Visit (Out-of-Network):    40% coinsurance
Inpatient Mental Health (In-Network):               20% coinsurance (after deductible)
Inpatient Substance Use Treatment (In-Network):     20% coinsurance (after deductible)

Mental health benefits are provided at parity with medical/surgical benefits
under the Mental Health Parity and Addiction Equity Act (MHPAEA).

===========================================================================
REFERRALS
===========================================================================
This is a PPO plan. Referrals to specialists are NOT required.
Members may self-refer to any in-network specialist without prior authorization,
except for services that require prior authorization (see below).

===========================================================================
PRIOR AUTHORIZATION REQUIREMENTS
===========================================================================
The following services require prior authorization from the plan before services
are rendered. Failure to obtain prior authorization may result in denial.

- Inpatient hospital admissions (non-emergency)
- Inpatient mental health and substance use disorder treatment
- Outpatient surgical procedures in select categories
- Advanced imaging (MRI, CT scan, PET scan) — select diagnoses
- Durable Medical Equipment (DME) over $500
- Home health care (more than 20 visits per plan year)
- Skilled nursing facility (SNF) stays
- Transplant services
- Specialty biologic drugs (Tier 4 and above)
- Out-of-network care when an in-network alternative is available (non-emergency)

Prior authorization decisions are made within:
  - Urgent/concurrent requests: 72 hours
  - Standard requests: 3 business days
  - Retrospective review: 30 calendar days

To request prior authorization, contact the plan's utilization management department
or submit via the provider portal. Members may not submit prior auth requests directly.

===========================================================================
NETWORK TIERS
===========================================================================
PREFERRED IN-NETWORK (Tier 1):  Contracted preferred providers. Lowest cost-share.
STANDARD IN-NETWORK (Tier 2):   Contracted standard providers. Standard cost-share as shown above.
OUT-OF-NETWORK:                  Non-contracted providers. Highest cost-share; member
                                  responsible for balance billing above allowed amount.

To verify network status for a specific provider, use the online provider directory
or call the member services number on the back of the ID card.

===========================================================================
COORDINATION OF BENEFITS (COB)
===========================================================================
When a member has coverage under more than one health plan, this plan follows
the standard COB rules:

- The primary plan pays first up to its benefit limits.
- The secondary plan may pay some or all of the remaining balance.
- The member's total reimbursement across both plans cannot exceed 100% of
  the allowed charge.
- If this plan is secondary, benefits are calculated based on what this plan
  would have paid had it been primary, minus what the primary plan paid.

Birthday Rule: For dependent children covered under two plans, the plan of the
parent whose birthday falls earlier in the calendar year is primary.

===========================================================================
EXCLUDED SERVICES (NOT COVERED)
===========================================================================
The following services are not covered under this plan:

- Cosmetic surgery (unless medically necessary due to injury or disease)
- Routine dental care (cleanings, fillings, extractions)
- Routine vision care (exams for glasses or contact lenses)
- Hearing aids and routine hearing exams
- Custodial care and long-term care
- Weight loss surgery (unless meeting medical necessity criteria — requires prior auth)
- Fertility treatments beyond diagnosis (IVF, surrogacy, donor services)
- Experimental or investigational treatments (as determined by the plan)
- Services not medically necessary as defined in the plan documents
- Services received outside the United States (except emergency care)
- Workers' compensation-covered services

For a complete list of exclusions, refer to the Evidence of Coverage document.

===========================================================================
CLAIMS SUBMISSION
===========================================================================
In-network providers submit claims directly to the plan on the member's behalf.

Out-of-network claims may be submitted by the member:
  - Filing deadline: 365 days from date of service
  - Submit to: Claims Department, P.O. Box indicated on the back of ID card
  - Required: itemized bill (CPT codes, diagnosis codes, provider NPI, date of service)
  - Reimbursement timeline: within 30 calendar days of receipt of a clean claim

EXPLANATION OF BENEFITS (EOB): Members receive an EOB after each claim is processed.
The EOB is not a bill. It shows: amount billed, allowed amount, plan payment, and
amount the member may owe.

===========================================================================
APPEALS AND GRIEVANCES
===========================================================================
Members have the right to appeal coverage decisions.

FIRST-LEVEL APPEAL: Submit within 180 days of the adverse benefit determination.
  Decision timeline: 60 days (standard), 72 hours (expedited/urgent).

SECOND-LEVEL APPEAL: If the first-level appeal is denied, submit a second-level appeal.
  Decision timeline: 60 days.

EXTERNAL REVIEW: After exhausting internal appeals, members may request an independent
external review through the state insurance department.

GRIEVANCES: Non-coverage complaints (e.g., access to care, provider behavior) may be
filed at any time. Resolution timeline: 30 calendar days.

All appeals and grievances must be handled by the plan's Appeals & Grievances department.
Member service representatives should not make coverage determinations — escalate to the
appropriate clinical or appeals team.
