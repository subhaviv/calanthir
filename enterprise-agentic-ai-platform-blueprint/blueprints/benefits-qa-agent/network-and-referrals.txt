NETWORK GUIDANCE AND REFERRAL POLICY — PPO PLAN 2026

===========================================================================
PPO NETWORK — OVERVIEW
===========================================================================

This plan uses a Preferred Provider Organization (PPO) network. Key features:

1. NO REFERRAL REQUIRED: Members can see any specialist, including out-of-network
   providers, without a referral from a primary care physician. This is a defining
   characteristic of a PPO plan and distinguishes it from an HMO or EPO.

2. IN-NETWORK SAVINGS: Members receive the highest level of benefits when using
   in-network (contracted) providers. In-network providers have agreed to accept
   the plan's allowed amount, which means lower out-of-pocket costs and no balance
   billing risk.

3. OUT-OF-NETWORK COVERAGE: Members may use out-of-network providers, but at
   higher cost-share. Out-of-network providers may also balance-bill — charge
   above the plan's allowed amount — and the member is responsible for the difference.

4. NO PRIMARY CARE PHYSICIAN (PCP) REQUIREMENT: Members are not required to select
   or register a primary care physician. However, having a regular PCP is
   encouraged for continuity of care.

===========================================================================
NETWORK TIERS
===========================================================================

TIER 1 — PREFERRED IN-NETWORK (lowest cost-share)
  Providers in the preferred tier have negotiated enhanced rates. Members pay
  the standard in-network cost-share shown in the SBC ($30 PCP / $60 specialist).

TIER 2 — STANDARD IN-NETWORK
  Contracted providers not in the preferred tier. Cost-share is the same as
  Tier 1 under this plan's benefit design (single in-network tier).

OUT-OF-NETWORK
  Non-contracted providers. Members pay higher coinsurance (40%) and may face
  balance billing. The plan's allowed amount is based on the Usual, Customary,
  and Reasonable (UCR) rate for that service in the geographic area.

===========================================================================
HOW TO VERIFY NETWORK STATUS
===========================================================================

Members and MSRs can verify provider network status using:
  - Online provider directory: available on the member portal (search by name, NPI, specialty, location)
  - Provider services phone line: call the number on the back of the member ID card
  - Real-time eligibility check (for MSRs): available in the member services system

IMPORTANT: Network status can change. Always verify at time of service. Listing
in the directory does not guarantee that the provider is currently accepting
new patients.

To confirm network status for a specific provider:
  1. Search by NPI (National Provider Identifier) for the most accurate match
  2. Confirm the service address matches (a provider may be in-network at one
     location but not another)
  3. Confirm the tax ID / group NPI for group practices

===========================================================================
SPECIALIST ACCESS — NO REFERRAL REQUIRED
===========================================================================
Under this PPO plan, members may self-refer to any specialty, including:
  - Dermatology
  - Orthopedics and sports medicine
  - Cardiology
  - OB/GYN (including for annual well-woman exams)
  - Mental health and behavioral health specialists
  - Physical therapy and occupational therapy (up to 30 visits per plan year in-network)
  - Chiropractic care (up to 20 visits per plan year in-network)
  - Ophthalmology (medical eye care, not routine vision — routine vision is not covered)

EXCEPTIONS — SERVICES THAT REQUIRE PRIOR AUTHORIZATION even without a referral:
  See the SBC prior authorization list. Prior authorization is required for
  inpatient admissions, advanced imaging, select surgical procedures, and
  specialty biologic drugs regardless of whether the provider is in-network.
  Prior auth protects members from unexpected non-covered charges. Members
  should confirm PA status before elective procedures.

===========================================================================
EMERGENCY AND URGENT CARE
===========================================================================
EMERGENCY CARE:
  Emergency services are covered at the in-network benefit level at any facility,
  in-network or out-of-network, when a reasonable person would believe a medical
  emergency exists. The $350 ER copay applies.

  After stabilization, if the member is admitted to an out-of-network hospital,
  the in-network benefit level continues for the duration of the admission for
  the emergency condition under No Surprises Act protections.

URGENT CARE:
  Use an in-network urgent care center for non-emergency conditions requiring
  prompt attention (infections, minor injuries, flu symptoms). Cost: $75 copay.
  Going to the ER for non-emergency conditions will still apply the $350 ER copay.

TELEHEALTH:
  Telehealth visits with in-network providers are covered at the primary care
  or specialist copay depending on the provider type. Use the plan's telehealth
  partner (listed on the member ID card) for the lowest cost-share.

===========================================================================
BALANCE BILLING PROTECTIONS
===========================================================================
For in-network providers: Members cannot be balance-billed. Providers must
accept the contracted allowed amount as payment in full.

For emergency services at any facility: Protections under the No Surprises Act
prevent balance billing for emergency services, air ambulance from non-network
providers, and services from non-network providers at in-network facilities
(unless the member provided informed consent in non-emergency situations).

If a member receives an unexpected bill from an in-network provider or for
emergency services, advise them to contact member services for assistance.
Do not advise members to pay disputed balance bills before the dispute is resolved.
