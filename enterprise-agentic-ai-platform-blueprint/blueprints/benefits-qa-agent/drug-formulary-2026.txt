PRESCRIPTION DRUG FORMULARY — PPO PLAN 2026
Effective: January 1, 2026

This formulary lists the prescription drugs covered under your PPO plan and
their assigned cost-share tier. The formulary is subject to change during the
plan year. Updated formularies are posted on the member portal.

===========================================================================
PHARMACY BENEFIT OVERVIEW
===========================================================================

PHARMACY DEDUCTIBLE: $200 individual / $400 family (separate from medical deductible)
PHARMACY OUT-OF-POCKET MAXIMUM: Counts toward the combined medical out-of-pocket maximum.

RETAIL PHARMACY (30-day supply):
  Tier 1 — Preferred Generic:         $10 copay
  Tier 2 — Non-Preferred Generic:     $25 copay
  Tier 3 — Preferred Brand:           $60 copay (after pharmacy deductible)
  Tier 4 — Non-Preferred Brand:       $90 copay (after pharmacy deductible)
  Tier 5 — Specialty:                 25% coinsurance, max $250/fill (after pharmacy deductible)

MAIL-ORDER PHARMACY (90-day supply, up to 2.5x the 30-day cost-share):
  Tier 1 — Preferred Generic:         $25 copay
  Tier 2 — Non-Preferred Generic:     $62.50 copay
  Tier 3 — Preferred Brand:           $150 copay (after pharmacy deductible)
  Tier 4 — Non-Preferred Brand:       $225 copay (after pharmacy deductible)
  Tier 5 — Specialty:                 25% coinsurance, max $625/fill (not available by mail)

SPECIALTY DRUGS: Available only at designated specialty pharmacies. A 30-day supply limit
applies. Prior authorization is required for all Tier 5 specialty drugs.

===========================================================================
STEP THERAPY REQUIREMENTS
===========================================================================
Some drugs require step therapy — you must try a preferred drug first before
the plan will cover the non-preferred alternative at the lower tier rate.

Common step therapy requirements:
  - Proton pump inhibitors: Try omeprazole (Tier 1) before pantoprazole (Tier 2) or Nexium (Tier 4)
  - SSRI antidepressants: Try sertraline (Tier 1) before escitalopram (Tier 2)
  - GLP-1 agonists: Try Trulicity (Tier 3) before Ozempic (Tier 4) for diabetes indication
  - TNF-alpha inhibitors: Try one biosimilar before branded Humira for applicable conditions

Step therapy overrides require a physician exception request submitted to the pharmacy
benefit manager. Processing time: 3–5 business days.

===========================================================================
QUANTITY LIMITS
===========================================================================
Quantity limits apply to certain medications to ensure safe and appropriate use.
Examples:
  - Opioid analgesics: 7-day supply for new prescriptions (acute pain)
  - Sumatriptan (migraine): 9 tablets per 30 days
  - Erectile dysfunction drugs: 6 tablets per 30 days
  - Hypnotic sleep aids: 30 tablets per 30 days

===========================================================================
EXCLUDED DRUGS
===========================================================================
The following categories are not covered under the pharmacy benefit:
  - Cosmetic drugs (minoxidil for cosmetic hair loss, tretinoin for cosmetic use)
  - Weight loss drugs (except when prescribed for diabetes per formulary exception)
  - Fertility drugs
  - Drugs available over-the-counter without a prescription (unless specifically listed)
  - Compounded drugs not approved by the FDA
  - Experimental or investigational drugs

===========================================================================
PRIOR AUTHORIZATION — PHARMACY
===========================================================================
Prior authorization (PA) is required for:
  - All Tier 5 specialty drugs
  - Select Tier 4 non-preferred brands
  - GLP-1 drugs for weight loss indication
  - Opioids exceeding quantity limits
  - Any drug requiring step therapy where prior therapy has not been documented

PA requests are submitted by the prescribing provider, not the member.
PA approval is valid for 12 months unless otherwise specified.

For questions about pharmacy benefits, cost-share, or formulary placement,
contact the Pharmacy Benefit helpline listed on the member ID card.
Member service representatives should not make PA decisions — route to the
clinical prior authorization team.
