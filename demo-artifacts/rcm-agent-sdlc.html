🔒 The key beat

deployed ≠ discoverable
Node 6 is the pivot: the BU’s agent is deployed and running in their own account — but nothing org-wide can find or use it yet. That autonomy stops exactly where governance begins. Being usable across the org is earned only by passing the registry’s intake (7) and a human curator’s approval (9).

The 4 blocking gates — and who owns each

each fails independently · none bypassable by the BU
  • ⛔ SCPsPlatform orgOrganizations service control policies — structural floor across every lane. Hardest to bypass; the account cannot override them even as admin.
  • ⛔ ConformancePlatform CI/CDSynth-time checks (~28 phase tests) — fail automatically at cdk synth, before anything deploys. No human.
  • ⛔ Schema intakeRegistryValidates the submitted record against the descriptor schema — auto-rejects drifted / tampered / wildcard records.
  • ✅ Approval👤 CuratorRecord enters DRAFT and a human platform curator must APPROVE before it is discoverable — the one human gate.
ⓘ Enforcement is layered three ways: pre-deploy (conformance gates at synth), post-deploy (AWS Config conformance packs, continuous drift detection), and runtime (Cedar / guardrails / kill-switch, per invocation). Only the pre-deploy gate changes shape by IaC tool — SCPs, intake and approval are tool-agnostic.
